I am a graduate student at Sun Yat-sen University interested in speech understanding and audio-language models. My research explores how models can reason about both what people say and how they say it.

## Research interests

- **Audio-language models:** Combining spoken content with acoustic cues such as pauses, changes in speaking rate, and interruptions.
- **Speech processing:** Transcribing conversations with background noise, regional accents, and overlapping speakers.
- **Applications:** Using speech understanding to help detect telecom fraud.
